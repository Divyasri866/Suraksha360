About Suraksha360

Discreet, reliable, and instantaneous protection engineered for high-risk situations.

Suraksha360 is an innovative women's safety ecosystem that combines physical wearable hardware and software to ensure real-time protection. Disguised as standard rubber bangles, the system offers an unassuming appearance that avoids detection by potential aggressors while providing instant emergency escalation.

Core Features

Engineered hardware and software mechanisms designed for real-world emergency response.

Magnetic Proximity Trigger

Dual rubber bangles equipped with embedded distance sensors detect when hands are forcibly separated, automatically initiating emergency escalation without pressing buttons.

Live Geolocation Broadcast

High-accuracy GPS tracking streams live coordinates, heading, altitude, and velocity directly to emergency services and trusted contacts.

Stealth Disguise Mode

Conceals the safety interface behind a fully functional Tic Tac Toe mini-game with a secret switch to ensure aggressors cannot detect the security portal.

Emergency Services Directory

Direct contact and navigation access to 14 verified police stations across Visakhapatnam, including Disha Mahila and Women Police Stations.

Family & Friends Hotline

Instant one-touch dialing, dynamic contact additions, and automated SMS dispatch to designated emergency family members.

Verified Incident Logging

Comprehensive incident event logging with timestamped alert history and fine-grained notification preference controls.

How It Works

Simple 4-step emergency readiness workflow for seamless peace of mind.

1

Open Settings Hub

Navigate to the Settings dashboard to manage your emergency profile and safe-haven home address.

2

Register Emergency Contacts

Add trusted family and friends phone numbers for instant one-touch emergency escalation.

3

Enable Geolocation

Grant location permissions so Suraksha360 can monitor and stream coordinates in high-accuracy mode.

4

Automatic Defense

Wear the dual bangles. If separated in danger, an automated SOS alert with GPS coordinates is dispatched.
